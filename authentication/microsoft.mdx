---
title: "Sign in with Microsoft to play Minecraft"
description: "Use your Microsoft account to access your licensed Minecraft: Java Edition profile in TecniLauncher, with skin display and online-mode server support."
---

Microsoft login connects TecniLauncher to your licensed Minecraft: Java Edition account. Once signed in, your username and skin are displayed in the launcher, and you can join servers that require a genuine (online-mode) account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the launcher and click Microsoft Login">
    Launch TecniLauncher and click **Microsoft Login** (also labeled **Sign in with Microsoft** on some screens).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your Microsoft account">
    A browser window opens to Microsoft's sign-in page. Enter the credentials for the Microsoft account linked to your Minecraft purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize TecniLauncher">
    Follow the prompts to authorize the launcher. TecniLauncher uses [CmlLib](https://github.com/CmlLib/CmlLib.Core), an open-source authentication library. Your password is never stored by the launcher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your profile">
    Once authorized, your Minecraft username and skin appear in the launcher. Your skin is fetched from [Minotar](https://minotar.net).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your session token is saved locally at `%APPDATA%\.TecniLauncher\Data\tcl_session.json`. This means you won't need to sign in again on the next launch — TecniLauncher attempts silent re-authentication automatically at startup.
</Note>

## Signing out

Click your profile avatar or account name in the launcher, then select **Sign out**. This deletes the saved session file at `%APPDATA%\.TecniLauncher\Data\tcl_session.json`.

## Requirements

You must have a valid **Minecraft: Java Edition** license tied to the Microsoft account you sign in with. If your account does not own Minecraft, authentication will succeed but the game will not launch.
