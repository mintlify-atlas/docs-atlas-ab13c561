---
title: "Play Minecraft offline without an account"
description: "Play singleplayer and LAN worlds in TecniLauncher without a Microsoft account. A consistent UUID is generated from your username so your progress persists."
---

Offline mode lets you play singleplayer and LAN worlds without a Microsoft account. TecniLauncher generates a consistent offline UUID derived from your chosen username using the same MD5-based algorithm Minecraft uses, so your player identity stays stable across sessions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the launcher and select offline mode">
    Launch TecniLauncher and click **Offline Mode** or **Play Offline**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a username">
    Type any username you want to appear in-game. If you leave the field empty, the launcher uses `Jugador` as the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the game">
    Click **Play** to start Minecraft with your offline profile. No account verification is performed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Offline mode only works on servers that have `online-mode` disabled. You cannot join servers that require a genuine premium Minecraft account. Skin display is not available in offline mode.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Your UUID is deterministic — the same username always produces the same UUID. This means your inventory and progress in singleplayer worlds are preserved across sessions as long as you keep the same username.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use a consistent username every time you play offline. Changing your username changes your UUID, which makes you appear as a new player in existing singleplayer worlds.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my offline username later?">
    Yes, you can change your offline username at any time. However, because the UUID is derived from the username, a different username produces a different UUID. In an existing singleplayer world, you will appear as a new player and will not have access to your previous inventory or player data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
