---
title: "Sign in with TecniStudio account"
description: "TecniStudio is TecniLauncher's own account system with skin support. Sign in with email and password — no Minecraft license required."
---

TecniStudio is TecniLauncher's own account system. It gives you a persistent username and skin without needing a paid Minecraft license, and works with TecniStudio-compatible servers. Credentials are authenticated against the TecniStudio service, and your profile information is fetched automatically after sign-in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click TecniStudio Login">
    In the launcher, click **TecniStudio Login**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter your TecniStudio email address and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for authentication">
    The launcher authenticates against the TecniStudio service and fetches your username and skin from your profile. Your session is saved to `%APPDATA%\.TecniLauncher\Data\tecni_session.json`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your profile">
    Once signed in, your username and skin appear in the launcher. The skin is fetched from TecniStudio's custom skin server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  TecniStudio accounts are separate from Microsoft and Mojang accounts. You cannot use a TecniStudio account on servers that require a genuine premium (online-mode) Minecraft account.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Skin display works automatically for TecniStudio accounts. To upload or change your skin, use the TecniStudio web portal.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you encounter login errors, verify that your TecniStudio email and password are correct and that you have an active internet connection.
</Tip>
