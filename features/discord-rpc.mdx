title: "Discord Rich Presence in TecniLauncher"
description: "TecniLauncher integrates with Discord Rich Presence to show your current Minecraft profile, mod loader, and version in your Discord status automatically."
---

TecniLauncher integrates with Discord Rich Presence to display your current Minecraft session in your Discord profile. Friends can see which profile you're playing, the mod loader, and the Minecraft version — all updated automatically as you switch profiles or start playing.

## What Discord shows

While TecniLauncher is running and you have an active session, your Discord status displays:

- **Profile name** — the name of the profile currently selected or in use.
- **Loader type** — the mod loader in use (for example, Fabric or Forge).
- **Minecraft version** — the version of Minecraft associated with the profile.

Your status updates automatically when you start a session or switch to a different profile. Discord also calculates and displays elapsed play time from the moment Rich Presence started.

## Requirements

- The **Discord desktop application** must be installed and running on your computer.
- No additional configuration is needed inside TecniLauncher — Rich Presence activates automatically when both applications are open.

<Note>
Discord Rich Presence requires the Discord desktop application. The web version of Discord does not support Rich Presence.
</Note>

## Disabling Rich Presence

Rich Presence is active whenever TecniLauncher is open and the Discord desktop app is running. To stop broadcasting your status, close Discord — Rich Presence stops as soon as Discord is no longer running.
