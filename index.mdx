TecniLauncher is a free, open-source Minecraft launcher for Windows built for players who want a clean, modern experience. It supports both premium (Microsoft) and offline play, lets you manage multiple profiles with different mod loaders, and puts thousands of mods from Modrinth a single click away.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the installer">
    Grab the latest `TecniLauncher_Setup.exe` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Tecnikero/TecniLauncher/releases) and run it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Choose **Microsoft login** to use your premium account, or select **Offline mode** to play without one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a profile">
    Click **New profile**, pick a Minecraft version and mod loader (Vanilla, Forge, Fabric, or NeoForge), and allocate RAM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play">
    Hit **Play** and TecniLauncher handles the rest — downloading game files, applying your loader, and launching Minecraft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  TecniLauncher runs on Windows only and requires .NET 10 or later (bundled with the installer).
</Note>
