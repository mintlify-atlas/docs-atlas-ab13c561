description: "Log in, create your first Minecraft profile, and launch the game in a few steps — TecniLauncher handles Java, downloads, and mod loaders automatically."
---

Once TecniLauncher is installed, you're a few steps away from playing Minecraft. This guide walks you through logging in, creating a profile, and launching the game for the first time. The whole process takes about five minutes, plus download time for Minecraft itself.

<Note>
  Java is managed automatically by TecniLauncher. You do not need to install Java separately — the launcher downloads the correct version for each Minecraft release.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Launch TecniLauncher">
    Open TecniLauncher from your Start menu or desktop shortcut. On first launch it initializes your data directory and checks for any launcher updates automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a login method">
    TecniLauncher supports three authentication options. For most players, Microsoft or Offline is the right choice:

    - **[Microsoft login](/authentication/microsoft)** — Sign in with your Microsoft account. Required to play on premium servers and access content tied to your Minecraft purchase.
    - **[Offline mode](/authentication/offline)** — Play without any account. Choose a username and you're in. Works for singleplayer and LAN sessions, but not for online servers that require a premium account.
    - **TecniStudio** — A custom account type for TecniStudio platform users.

    Select your preferred method and complete the sign-in flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first profile">
    Click **New Profile** to open the profile creation screen. Fill in the following:

    - **Name** — Any name you like, used to identify the profile in the launcher.
    - **Minecraft version** — Choose the version you want to play. Stick with the latest stable release if you're unsure.
    - **Loader** — Select **Vanilla** for a standard Minecraft experience with no mod loader. You can create additional profiles with Forge, Fabric, or NeoForge later.
    - **RAM allocation** — The default is 2 GB. For a smooth Vanilla experience, 2–4 GB is sufficient.

    <Tip>
      Don't over-allocate RAM. Minecraft needs memory, but so does your operating system and other apps. A good rule of thumb: leave at least 2–3 GB free for Windows. On a machine with 8 GB total RAM, allocating 4–5 GB to Minecraft is a safe maximum.
    </Tip>

    Click **Save** to create the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and play">
    Select your new profile from the launcher and click **Play**. If this is your first time launching that Minecraft version, TecniLauncher will download the game files first — expect around 300 MB for a fresh Vanilla installation. Progress is shown in the launcher.

    Once the download completes, Minecraft launches automatically. Your profile settings, mods folder, and saves are all stored separately under `%APPDATA%\.TecniLauncher\Instances\{profileName}\`, so each profile stays isolated.
  </Step>
</Steps>
